House Removals

Careful, well-organised house removals for flats, terraces and family homes of every size.

Moving home is one of life's biggest milestones, and it deserves a team that treats it that way. Our uniformed crews protect floors and doorways, wrap furniture, load in a planned order and place everything in the right room at your new address.

  • Dedicated move co-ordinator
  • Furniture blankets and floor protection
  • Dismantling and reassembly of beds and wardrobes
  • Fixed, itemised quotation before you book

Packing and Unpacking

Full or part packing using quality materials, completed by trained and careful packers.

Packing is the part of moving most people dread. Let our packers take it on: we bring double-walled boxes, wardrobe cartons, paper and bubble wrap, and pack room by room with clear labelling. Unpacking is available at the other end too.

  • Full, part or fragile-only packing
  • All materials supplied and delivered
  • Room-by-room labelling system
  • Unpacking and removal of used materials
