Our Story

From one van to a nationwide service

SwiftMove began with a single van, two friends and a simple idea: removals should be straightforward, fairly priced and carried out by people who care. Word spread quickly, and what started as weekend house moves soon grew into a full-time business.

More than a decade later, we operate a fleet of modern vehicles and crews across the country, offering packing, storage and commercial relocations alongside our house removals. We have grown, but our approach has not changed: plan carefully, communicate clearly and treat every customer's belongings as if they were our own.
