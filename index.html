FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Send us an enquiry and we'll be happy to help.

How much does a house removal cost in the UK?

The cost depends on the size of your property, the distance between addresses, access at each end and any extra services such as packing or storage. As a rough guide, a local move for a two-bedroom home is typically priced lower than a long-distance move for a four-bedroom house. We provide a free, no-obligation quotation with every cost itemised, so there are no surprises.

Do you provide packing materials?

Yes. We can supply sturdy boxes, wardrobe cartons, packing paper, bubble wrap and tape, either delivered ahead of your move for you to pack yourself or as part of our full packing service.

Are my belongings insured during the move?

Our removals include goods-in-transit and public liability cover as standard. Full details and cover limits are set out in your quotation, and we are happy to talk you through the options for high-value items.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking four to six weeks ahead where possible, particularly for Fridays, weekends and the end of the month. That said, we will always do our best to help with short-notice moves.

Do you offer weekend removals?

Yes. We carry out removals seven days a week, including Saturdays and Sundays, and can arrange evening moves for offices that need to avoid disruption during working hours.

Can I change my moving date?

Of course. Completion dates do change, and we understand that. Let us know as soon as you can and we will rearrange your move, subject to availability, in line with the terms in your quotation.
